# Protocol amendment: stop after full primary submission coverage

The original schedule called for 8 submissions × 2 passes = 16 reviews. After 12 reviews were frozen, the researcher decided before reveal to stop after one final review of `submission_A/pass_01`, because of evaluator execution and token cost. This review completes the primary dataset: all 8 final submissions have at least one independent blind post-hoc engineering review.

The remaining second-pass jobs are not evaluator failures and must not be run to reach 16/16. Previously completed double-pass submissions remain the evaluator reproducibility subset. The incomplete repeated-pass subset is by design under this amendment. The decision did not depend on `submission_A`'s true experimental condition or evaluation result. The historical 16-job schedule is retained unchanged.
